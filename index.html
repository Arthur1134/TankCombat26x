HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS • OBJECTIVES • TACTICS

PLAYER 1

A/D rotate • W/S drive • Mouse aim • Click fire • 1–5 weapons • Shift / E abilities

PLAYER 2 (CO-OP)

Arrow keys drive • Auto-aim • Enter fire • Digits 6–0 weapons • R-Shift / . abilities

Local co-op needs two keyboards / shared keyboard — not available on phones.

BOUNCE / RICOCHET

Most shells bounce off walls. Bank shots around corners — denser maps reward clever angles. Watch the bounce count; some weapons ricochet more.

5-LEVEL CAMPAIGN

  • L1 SEARCH & DESTROY — kill tank quota
  • L2 HOLD THE LINE — hold the zone under fire
  • L3 CLEAR THE COMPOUND — destroy marked buildings
  • L4 SECURE & HOLD — kill quota, then hold
  • L5 BOSS — destroy the giant tank

BUILDINGS & WEAPONS

Start with cannon. Smash buildings/armories to unlock MG, rocket, shotgun, tank-pong for the run (shared in co-op).

CHASSIS & ABILITIES

  • SCOUT — fast & fragile; Boost + Smoke
  • BALANCED — all-rounder; Repair + Cover barrier
  • HEAVY — slow armor; Shield + EMP stun

Upgrades between levels. ESC pauses. Good luck, commander.
